# Manual wheelchairs — documentation requirements

_Excerpt from published coverage policy. Abridged for this exercise; the structure and the
substance of the requirements are faithful._

## Covered codes

| Code | Description |
| --- | --- |
| K0001 | Standard wheelchair |
| K0002 | Standard hemi (low seat) wheelchair |
| K0003 | Lightweight wheelchair |
| K0004 | High strength, lightweight wheelchair |

## General requirements — all manual wheelchairs

A manual wheelchair is covered when all of the following are met and documented:

1. The beneficiary has a mobility limitation that significantly impairs their ability to
   participate in one or more mobility-related activities of daily living in the home.
2. The limitation cannot be sufficiently resolved by a cane or walker.
3. The beneficiary's home can accommodate the wheelchair.
4. **The beneficiary is able to self-propel the wheelchair, or has a caregiver who is
   available, willing, and able to provide assistance.**
5. A face-to-face encounter with the treating practitioner occurred within the six months
   prior to the written order, and the encounter note documents the mobility limitation.
6. A written order is signed and dated by the treating practitioner prior to delivery.

## Additional requirements — K0003 lightweight

A lightweight wheelchair (K0003) is covered **only if** the general requirements are met
**and**:

- The beneficiary can self-propel in a standard wheelchair but **cannot do so effectively**,
  or cannot self-propel in a standard wheelchair at all; **and**
- The beneficiary **can** self-propel effectively in a lightweight wheelchair.

**A K0003 is not covered where the medical record shows that propulsion is provided by a
caregiver.** In that circumstance the reduced weight confers no functional benefit to the
beneficiary and the standard wheelchair (K0001) is the appropriate code.

Claims for K0003 submitted against documentation that does not establish the above will be
denied, and are not correctable by resubmission without either amended documentation or a
corrected order.
