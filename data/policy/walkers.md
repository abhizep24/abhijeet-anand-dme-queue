# Walkers — documentation requirements

_Excerpt from published coverage policy. Abridged for this exercise._

## Covered codes

| Code | Description |
| --- | --- |
| E0135 | Walker, folding, adjustable or fixed height |
| E0143 | Walker, folding, wheeled, adjustable or fixed height |
| E0149 | Walker, heavy duty, wheeled |

## General requirements

A walker is covered when the beneficiary has a mobility limitation that significantly
impairs their ability to participate in mobility-related activities of daily living in the
home, and the limitation can be sufficiently resolved by a walker.

## Choosing the code

The code billed must match what the medical record supports.

- **E0135** is appropriate where the beneficiary can lift and advance the walker and does
  not require seated rest during ambulation.
- **E0143** is appropriate where the beneficiary cannot safely lift and advance a
  non-wheeled walker, or where the medical record documents a need for **intermittent
  seated rest** during household or community ambulation — for example, secondary to
  cardiopulmonary limitation.

## Written orders

The item dispensed must match the written order. A supplier may not substitute a different
item, including an upgrade supported by the medical record, without a corrected written
order signed by the treating practitioner. Delivery of an item other than the one ordered
is not billable.
